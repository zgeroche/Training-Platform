Privacy Policy

Training Platform

Effective November 15, 2026 · Personal, non-commercial app

In short

  • This app is used by one person: its developer, Zach, using a personal WHOOP account.
  • Data is stored only on the developer's personal computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising, or sent to analytics services.

1What this app is

Training Platform is a personal marathon-training tool. It combines WHOOP physiology data with running data from a Samsung Galaxy Watch so the developer can review training, recovery and sleep in one place. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups from other users.

2Data the app accesses

With the account owner's permission through WHOOP's official sign-in, the app reads:

  • Profile: name and email address
  • Body measurements: height, weight and maximum heart rate
  • Physiological cycles: day strain, energy expenditure and heart rate
  • Recovery: recovery score, resting heart rate, HRV, SpO2 and skin temperature
  • Sleep: sleep stages, performance, efficiency and respiratory rate
  • Workouts: strain, heart rate, heart-rate zones, energy and distance

Access is read-only. The app cannot change anything in a WHOOP account.

3How the data is used

Data is used only to show the account owner their own training history, trends and recovery context. It is not used to make decisions about anyone else.

4Where the data is stored

Data is stored in a local database on the developer's personal computer. The app's server listens only on that computer and is not reachable from the internet. WHOOP sign-in tokens are stored in the same local database.

5Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with any third party. The app has no advertising, tracking or analytics.

6Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the account owner deletes it. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data and tokens. Access can be revoked at any time by choosing Disconnect in the app, or by removing the app's access in WHOOP account settings.

7Changes

If the app starts using data in a new way, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
