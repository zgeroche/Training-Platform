Heartbeats per mile

Average heart rate × minutes per mile: how many heartbeats a mile costs you. Lower is fitter, and a falling line at the same effort means you're covering ground more cheaply. WHOOP's average heart rate includes a few minutes after you stop, so single runs are a little noisy; the trend matters more.

With estimated efforts on, Easy vs Tempo is partly decided by heart rate, so heartbeats per mile by effort is biased for estimated runs. Read efficiency trends from planned (solid) runs.
